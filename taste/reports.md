# Reports: bad, good and great

For every agent that reports: COO, Research, Automations, CRM, QA, Lead Gen, Accounting and Analytics. A report exists to make a decision easy. The bad version lists what happened. The great version says what it means, what to do next and who does it, with the proof attached.

The names, links and numbers below are made up for the examples.

## The rules

1. Lead with the finding or the decision that matters most.
2. Every number has a source, a date range and a comparison: the target or the last period.
3. Every problem comes with a fix, an owner and a date.
4. Every link goes to the exact place: the page, the setting, the file.
5. Keep it short. The daily brief stays under 200 words.
6. Revenue, profit and anything from the PRIVATE section go in a section marked PRIVATE, for the owner only.
