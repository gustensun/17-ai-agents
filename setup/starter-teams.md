# Starter teams

You can install all 17 agents, and most people get more done with 4 to 6. Start with the team that matches your path and your stage, use it for two weeks, and add an agent when a real bottleneck shows up. The COO recommends a team in stage 4 of setup, and you can change it any time by saying "add an agent".

| You are | Start with | Add next | Add it when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brand new, no offer yet | COO, CMO, Research, Copywriter | Content | you've picked an offer and want an audience |
| A founder selling a service | COO, CMO, Lead Gen, Copywriter, Sales | CRM | you have more leads than you can track by memory |
| Building a digital product | COO, CMO, Research, Copywriter, Funnel, Design | Coding | the product is a tool or an app |
| An affiliate | COO, CMO, Content, Creator, Copywriter | Analytics | you publish every week and want to know what earns |
| Scaling (the offer and sales already work) | COO, CMO, Analytics, Funnel, Copywriter, CRM, Automations, Support | Sales, QA | volume makes follow-up or quality slip |
| Running everything | All 17 | | |

## Brand new

**COO, CMO, Research, Copywriter.** You need an offer people want and words that sell it before anything else pays off.

- Week 1: Research builds the avatar grid and finds 10 outliers in your niche. The CMO uses them to shape your offer with the Winning Offer Formula.
- Week 2: the Copywriter writes your one-line pitch, an opt-in page for a freebie and 5 posts. The COO plans week 3.
- Add Content when you're ready to publish every week.

## Founder (you sell a service)

**COO, CMO, Lead Gen, Copywriter, Sales.** Clients come from conversations, so the team finds the right people and starts them.

- Week 1: the CMO positions the offer (Client Formula: Position). Lead Gen finds 25 leads that match your avatar, each with a visible problem you solve.
- Week 2: the Copywriter writes outreach and follow-ups from the DM closing scripts. Sales ranks replies and drafts the next message for each one. You approve and send.
- Add the CRM agent when the list outgrows your memory.

## Digital product

**COO, CMO, Research, Copywriter, Funnel, Design.** A product needs a validated idea, a page that sells it and a funnel that pays for its traffic.

- Week 1: Research checks demand (the winning products gallery and outliers). The CMO shapes the product with the Digital Product Fastlane: method, moat, market.
- Week 2: the Funnel agent maps a low-ticket funnel. The Copywriter writes the sales page from the 17P sections. Design sets the style block and lays out the page.
- Add Coding when the product is a tool, calculator or app.

## Affiliate

**COO, CMO, Content, Creator, Copywriter.** Affiliate income follows content that ranks and a bonus that makes people buy through your link.

- Week 1: the CMO picks the product and the niche. Creator chooses 5 to 10 creators to watch. Content finds outliers and plans 2 videos a week.
- Week 2: the Copywriter writes the first scripts, the bonus page and the follow-up emails.
- Add Analytics when you publish every week and want to see which videos bring referrals.

## Scaling

**COO, CMO, Analytics, Funnel, Copywriter, CRM, Automations, Support.** The offer works, so the job is finding the weakest number and fixing it, then keeping the machine clean.

- Week 1: Analytics finds the weakest level (reach, conversions, sales or ascend). The Funnel agent and the Copywriter fix that one first.
- Week 2: CRM cleans and tags the list. Automations builds the missing follow-ups. Support drafts replies to every new ticket.
- Add Sales when hot leads wait too long, and QA when launches go out with mistakes.

## Agents that need a connection

These agents do their best work once they can read a tool. Until then they work on whatever you paste in.

| Agent | Needs | Without it |
|---|---|---|
| Support | [INBOX] | Paste the messages, get drafted replies |
| Accounting | [INBOX], [STORAGE] | Upload receipts, get file names, categories and flags |
| CRM | [CRM] | Paste or export a contact list, get a cleaned list and stages |
| Automations | [FUNNEL SOFTWARE], [CRM], [EMAIL TOOL] | Get a build sheet to set up by hand |
| Analytics | [ANALYTICS], [PAYMENTS], [CHANNELS] | Paste the numbers, get the report |
| Coding | [CODE HOST], [HOSTING] | Get the code and the publish steps |
| Sales | [CRM], [INBOX] | Paste the conversations, get the ranking and the next message |
