# Outlier videos: a worked example for one niche

332 videos that pulled at least 2x the views of their channel's typical upload, from about 160 business and AI channels, published September 16 to November 15, 2026. It's a finished example of the list [outlier-research](../skills/outlier-research/SKILL.md) makes, and the 22 formats in that skill come from it. Use it to see what a good list looks like and how formats compare. The owner's customer watches other channels, so the owner's list always comes from a new run in the owner's niche.

The live version, with thumbnails, filters and sorting: [outlier videos gallery](https://outlier-videos.vibepreview.app/). The live site shows the titles, channels and thumbnails. This file keeps the counts by format and topic, so you can read it without opening the site.

## Rules

1. Run the owner's own list. Build the pool from the Creator's watchlist and the customer in Brain sections 1 and 3, as the skill says. This list is for a business and AI audience.
2. Never copy a title, a thumbnail or a script. Keep the format and the promise that worked, and use the owner's story, example, opinion and proof.
3. Views and multiples were public numbers on the day they were collected. They keep changing.
4. Formats 13, 16 and 22 make money claims. Use them only with real numbers from the proof bank, and follow house rule 11 in [brain/CORE.md](../brain/CORE.md).

## How to read the live gallery

- **Outlier multiple.** The video's views divided by the median views of the channel's latest uploads. 2x is the floor, and every video has at least 10,000 views.
- **Sort** by Outlier multiple, Most views, Newest or Views vs subs. **Filter** by topic or by format.
- **Possible paid promotion.** 10 videos carry this flag: extreme multiples on small channels, or titles built around a free trial. Their views likely came from ads. Leave them out of the top picks.
- Thumbnails and titles belong to their channel owners. Study the packaging and write the owner's own.

## What the list shows

- **Multiples.** Without the 10 flagged videos, the median outlier is 3.2x. 75 reached 5x or more, 18 reached 10x or more, and the highest was 32.2x.
- **Topics.** AI tools and agents 192, business and scaling 46, AI income 23, one marketing platform's tutorials and news 21, content and YouTube 18, SEO and search 14, paid ads 9, sales and clients 5, funnels and marketing 4.
- **Months.** September (from the 16th) 103, October 156, November (to the 15th) 73.

## Patterns to test in any niche

- **Something new.** 124 of the 322 are about something new: a tool tested, a news change or a platform shift. In another niche, new might be a rule change, a study, a product, a season or a trend the customer keeps hearing about.
- **Plain tutorials.** 37 outliers, and the highest multiple in the list (32.2x). A clear how-to for a task the customer has right now still works.
- **Story formats.** Start over (11), teardown with numbers (13), side income after hours (16) and extreme experiment (17) have higher medians than the list as a whole, on 5 to 9 videos each. Worth a test, with the owner's own story and proof.
- **One niche's platform.** The platform tutorial format has the highest median (7.3x), but it covers one software platform and 10 more videos in it were flagged as possible paid promotion. In the owner's niche, the same slot goes to the main tool or platform the customer uses.
