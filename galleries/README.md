## Rules for every gallery

1. **Use the patterns. Never copy.** Take the structure, the order, the price range and the kind of bonus or guarantee. Leave the names, the words, the photos, the prices and the testimonials. The owner's version comes from the Brain, in the owner's voice.
2. **The live sites name other businesses, people and tools.** The written indexes leave those out and use neutral words. When you show the owner a reference, link the live page and say what to borrow from it.
3. **Numbers in a gallery belong to someone else.** They are sellers' claims, made-up demo content or public estimates. Never repeat them as facts. The owner's numbers come from the proof bank and follow house rule 11 in [brain/CORE.md](../brain/CORE.md).
4. **The Brain wins.** When a gallery pattern doesn't fit the owner's customer, offer or Style block, follow the Brain and tell the owner why.

## Keep them current

The galleries were collected in November 2026. Markets move, so check a pattern against fresh research before the owner bets on it: [deep-market-research](../skills/deep-market-research/SKILL.md) for offers and prices, [outlier-research](../skills/outlier-research/SKILL.md) for content. Save the owner's own references in `my-business/research/` with a link and a note on what to borrow.
