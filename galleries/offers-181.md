# Offer gallery: 181 offers

181 offers sold online, sorted by category and price tier. Use it when you create or improve an offer, set a price, stack bonuses or choose a guarantee. It shows what the market sells. The owner's offer still comes from the Brain.

The live version, with filters: [offer gallery](https://offervault.vibepreview.app/). The live site names the businesses. This file leaves out offer names, owners, brands and tool names, so you can read it without opening the site.

## Rules

1. Use the patterns. Never copy a promise, a bonus name, a price or the wording of a guarantee. Write the owner's offer from the Brain, in the owner's voice.
2. Every number here is the seller's own claim. Prices, list prices, stated values, results and guarantees were collected as written on each sales page and were not checked. Never repeat them as facts.
3. Income and result claims in the owner's copy follow house rule 11 in [brain/CORE.md](../brain/CORE.md): the claim must be in the proof bank, with the disclaimer next to it.
4. Health, money and dating offers here make big claims. The owner's copy makes only the claims the owner can prove.

## How to use it

1. Read Brain section 3 (the customer) and section 5 (the offer and the value ladder).
2. Pick the tier you're working on and read its patterns below.
3. Find 3 to 5 offers in the closest category. If there's no close category, use the same type and tier from any category.
4. Note what they share: the type, the price, what's included, the guarantee and how the price is anchored.
5. Shape the owner's offer with [winning-offer-formula](../skills/winning-offer-formula/SKILL.md), using the owner's real numbers, proof and words. Tell the owner which offers you compared, by number (for example #87).

## How the tiers work

- Low ticket: under $100 one-time, or under $50 a month.
- Mid ticket: $100 to $999 one-time, or $50 to $299 a month.
- High ticket: $1,000 or more one-time, $300 or more a month, or sold through an application or a call.
- The tier uses the entry price, the cheapest paid way in. Yearly plans count per month. Prices in other currencies were converted to approximate US dollars to set the tier, and the price shown is the original.

## What each tier does

### Low ticket: 101 offers

- **Price.** The median one-time price is $27, and $27 is also the most common price (14 offers). 22 of the 68 one-time offers cost under $10. Those are priced to buy customers and pay for ads, and the profit comes from the next steps in the ladder.
- **Product.** 61 of the 101 are books or courses that promise one specific result, often with a timeframe: "train your dog at home in under 2 weeks", "launch a profitable online store in 7 days". Health, fitness and dating add 12 apps, most of them sold as yearly plans.
- **Bonuses.** The repeat stack: a private community (39 offers), templates, scripts or swipe files (35), a fast start or quick start video series (12) and a calculator or checklist (11).
- **Anchor.** 50 show a list price, and the median offer is 81% off it. 51 state a total value, and the median stated value is 25x the price.
- **Risk.** 51 have a money-back guarantee or a refund promise, most often 30 days with no questions asked (31 offers). 11 more offer a free or $1 trial. Five go to 60 days and one to 365 days to stand out.

### Mid ticket: 43 offers

- **Access.** 29 are subscriptions with a median price of $97 a month, mostly paid communities (17) and software (8). The 14 one-time offers are mostly deeper courses from $117 to $999.
- **Live time.** 19 include live calls, Q&As or coaching. That live time is the main difference from the low tier.
- **A cheap first step.** 14-day trials, a first month for $39 or $50, and founding prices ("$97/mo founder; $197/mo standard").
- **Templates as the bonus layer.** Account templates, prompts, scripts and extra courses come bundled with the membership.
- **Risk.** 19 offer a guarantee or a trial. Three tie the refund to doing the work: finishing the setup, doing the outreach or completing the plan.

### High ticket: 37 offers

- **Done for them, or done with them.** 22 are done-for-you services and 8 are coaching. The rest are events (4), courses (2) and one community.
- **The price is often hidden.** 18 are sold through an application or a call. The public prices run $1,500 to $6,300 one-time, $319 to $4,000 a month, and $3,999 to $23,500 a year for health memberships.
- **Guarantees are rare.** 5 guarantee something, and 4 of those guarantee a result: revenue reached, new clients each month, ad results beaten by 30% or a set number of matches.
- **Health and relationships sell access and certainty.** Body scans, longevity memberships, coach certifications, a 3-day couples intensive and matchmaking with a set number of matches.

### Build the ladder

A low-ticket book or course with a fast start series and a community brings in buyers. A monthly membership with live calls keeps them. An application-only, done-with-you offer serves the buyers who want the result faster. Compare the owner's value ladder in Brain section 5 with this, and name the missing step.
