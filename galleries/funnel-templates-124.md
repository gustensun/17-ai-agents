# Funnel and website templates (124)

Live gallery: [foundertemplates.vibepreview.app](https://foundertemplates.vibepreview.app/). Filter by niche and funnel type, preview every page of a funnel and open the live pages.

This file is the written index of the same 124 templates, so an agent can choose references without opening the site. Each entry gives the niche, the pages, the look, the type and the demo offer chain, with a link to the live template.

## Rules

1. A template is a reference for structure and taste. Take the page flow, the section order, where proof sits, the layout idea and the mood. Leave the copy, names, photos, numbers, testimonials and the signature effect. The full list is in [what you may take](../skills/conversion-design/references/directions.md#references-what-you-may-take).
2. Every brand, person, price, number and testimonial in the demos is made up or a placeholder in square brackets. None of it goes on a live page. Real claims come from the proof bank in the Brain and follow house rule 11 in [CORE.md](../brain/CORE.md).
3. Pick by funnel type first, then by look, then by niche. A template from another niche with the right flow beats a same-niche template with the wrong flow.
4. The owner's Style block (Brain section 10) wins. When a template's look fights the brand, keep the structure and change the look.
5. The demos don't send forms or take payments. Connect the real tools from the Tool map and test the whole path before launch.

## How to pick references

1. Get the funnel type from [funnel-strategy](../skills/funnel-strategy/SKILL.md) and the page map from [funnel-types](../skills/funnel-types/SKILL.md).
2. Open that type's section below and shortlist three: one close to the owner's niche, one close to the Style block, and one from another niche that does something the other two don't.
3. For each, write one to three things to borrow and what to leave.
4. Show the owner the three links with those notes and let them pick. Put the pick and the reasons in the build brief.
5. Build with [conversion-design](../skills/conversion-design/SKILL.md) and check the result against [taste/design-ai-vs-designed.md](../taste/design-ai-vs-designed.md).

## What the library shows

- **Funnel types.** Website and lead funnels 33, websites 20, sales pages 20, VSL application 11, automated cart 11, ecom 8, webinar cart 8, challenge 7, low ticket 6.
- **Niches.** Local business 32, digital products and courses 27, software 17, coaches 12, consultants and experts 11, ecommerce 7, speakers and events 6, creators 5, agencies and studios 4, authors 3.
- **Flows.** 82 templates are full funnels and 42 are single pages. Every full funnel has a thank-you page. 35 book a call or a visit on a calendar step, 38 take a payment on an order form or checkout, 23 offer an upsell and 7 a second upsell. 38 demo offers include an order bump and 76 name the next step after the signup or purchase.
- **One idea per page.** Most templates are built around one idea from their own world: the arch of a clinic, a draggable roof before and after, a seat counter that fills on a cohort page, a book that opens in 3D on scroll. That's the "one motif" and the "signature moment" in [conversion-design](../skills/conversion-design/SKILL.md). Borrow the habit of having one, then find the owner's own.
- **Type.** 108 of the 124 pair two or more families and 16 use one. 63 use a serif and 35 include a mono face. The most used families: IBM Plex Mono (10), Hanken Grotesk (10), Fraunces (9), Switzer (9), Bricolage Grotesque (9), Instrument Serif (9), Inter (8) and JetBrains Mono (8).
