# The Affiliate Fastlane

Recommend one software platform as the operating system for your audience's business, and earn recurring commission without client delivery. Refer once, and get paid every month the account stays active. In this file, [PLATFORM] is the software the owner recommends (Brain section 10, Tool map).

## The formula: System, Showcase, Support

| Phase | Steps | In one line |
|---|---|---|
| System | 1 Audience, 2 Outcome, 3 Features | How [PLATFORM] will change their life |
| Showcase | 4 Channel, 5 Format, 6 Packaging | A YouTube channel that sells around the clock |
| Support | 7 Onboarding, 8 Education, 9 Community | An ecosystem that helps them stay |

**1. Audience.** The specific type of business owner you help, and the person you become for them. For example: "the [PLATFORM] person for coaches", "the [PLATFORM] tutorial person for sales teams", "the software-business person".

**2. Outcome.** Sell the result they can get with [PLATFORM]. People don't care about features until they know how those features get them the outcome they want.

**3. Features.** The specific features that deliver that result, and how. Turn the features into a framework specific to your process. Ready-made templates bridge the gap and save them a lot of time.

**4. Channel.** Where you show up: YouTube, email or social. YouTube is the best fit, because videos rank for years and bring free traffic. Face or faceless: your personal brand, or a "[PLATFORM] tutorials" channel.

**5. Format.** The style of content you become known for: tutorials and reviews, demos and walkthroughs, new release updates and news, live builds from start to finish, behind the scenes of how you get clients, tech setups and free templates.

**6. Packaging.** Titles, thumbnails, the trial link and the reason to join through you. Make people curious enough that they click to get the answer.

| Title type | Pattern | Its job |
|---|---|---|
| Result | Start a [RESULT] [BUSINESS TYPE] with [PLATFORM] | Promise the outcome |
| Story | If I started over, here's how I'd grow to [GOAL] | Open a loop they need to close |
| Question | [PLATFORM] in [YEAR]: still worth it? | Make them click for the answer |

More patterns: "How to actually use [FEATURE] like a pro", "Big news: [PLATFORM] just changed [THING]". Result titles need a real result behind them (house rule 11).

**7. Onboarding.** Get them up and running as fast as possible. Pick one real setup step during the free trial, such as connecting their domain or launching their first page, and help every referral finish it.

**8. Education.** Trainings that help them use more of the platform. A clear step-by-step roadmap for making money with the software is often what separates people who cancel from people who stay.

**9. Community.** A place to ask questions, get help and share wins. People cancel over small things you could have answered in a minute.

## The 10K Affiliate Path: Attract, Convert, Retain

| Phase | Build | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Attract: get seen by the right people | One niche (pick one). Search videos: tutorials people type into YouTube. Comparisons: [PLATFORM] against the tools they pay for now. Shorts: quick wins that send people to the long videos. An email list: a free template in exchange for an email. Cadence: 2 videos a week, every week | A content engine that brings in leads while you sleep |
| Convert: make your link the obvious pick | A bonus: templates or training they can't get elsewhere. A bonus page: who it's for, what they get, why you. A trial link: tracked, short and in every description. A live build: build something real, then invite them. Follow-up: an email sequence. One call to action at the end of everything | A bonus offer that makes your link the easy yes |
| Retain: keep them winning, and paying | Onboarding: their first result live in 7 days. A private community just for your referrals. A weekly call with live builds and Q&A. Upgrades: show them when the bigger plan pays for itself. Check-ins: a personal message after day 3 and day 30. Wins: share results so nobody quits early | A support system that keeps referrals active for years |

Signups pay you once. Retention pays you every month.

| Day | Task | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Record | One search tutorial for your niche |

| Wed | Clip | Three shorts that point to the video |
| Thu | Publish | A second video: a comparison or a review |
| Fri | Support | A live call for your referrals |

## Stop this, do this

| Stop | Do |

| No bonus | A template that makes you the obvious pick |
| Forgetting them after signup | Onboard them, and keep them winning |

## The math

- Monthly pay per active referral = plan price × commission rate.
- Active referrals needed = monthly goal ÷ pay per referral, rounded up.

Example with one program's published numbers at the time of writing: a $297 a month plan at 40% recurring pays $118.80 a month per active referral.

| Monthly goal | Active referrals |
|---|---|
| $1,000 | 9 |
| $2,500 | 22 |
| $5,000 | 43 |
| $10,000 | 85 (85 × $118.80 = $10,098) |

Run the owner's real program terms before using any number in content.

> Income disclaimer: these numbers are examples based on one program's published commission and list prices at the time of writing. They are not a promise or guarantee of earnings. Your results depend on your effort, skills, audience, niche and market conditions, and many affiliates earn little or nothing. Commission rates, plans and prices can change at any time, so always check the program's current affiliate terms.

## Rules

- Disclose the affiliate link in every piece of content, as the platform and the advertising rules where you live require.
- Recommend only what the owner uses and would recommend without the commission.
- Bonus promises must be delivered. The Automations agent sets up the delivery before the bonus page goes live.
- House rule 11 applies to every income claim.
