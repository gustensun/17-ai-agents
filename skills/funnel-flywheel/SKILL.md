# The Funnel Flywheel

Seven steps, one metric each. Fix the weakest step first: a 10% gain on the weakest number usually beats a 10% gain on the strongest. Analytics finds the weakest step, the CMO picks the fix, the COO assigns it.

| Step | Metric | The question |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Find | TAM, total addressable market | Are enough of the right people seeing us? |
| 2. Convert | CVR, conversion rate | Do the people who see the offer buy it? |
| 3. Upsell | AOV, average order value | Does each buyer spend as much as the help they need? |
| 4. Cross sell | Short-term CLV, customer lifetime value | Do buyers come back for related things? |
| 5. Ascend | Long-term CLV | Do the best buyers move up to more help? |
| 6. Retain | CRR, customer retention rate | Do customers stay? |
| 7. Ignite | CAR, customer advocacy rate | Do customers bring other customers? |

## 1. Find more customers (TAM)

- Figure out where the market hangs out online.
- Use inbound or outbound marketing to get their attention.
- Create free stuff that wows them and does the selling for you.
- Automate the nurture so people ask to buy from you.

## 2. Convert more of them (CVR)

- Create better offers than anyone in the market ([winning-offer-formula](../winning-offer-formula/SKILL.md)).
- Give every offer its own sales page.
- Test headlines, hooks, videos, design and copy.
- Use heatmaps, click maps and split tests to see what works.
- Improve each step of the funnel, from the first page to the last.
- Survey buyers on why they bought, and non-buyers on why they didn't.

## 3. Upsell your customers (AOV)

- Works best after a low-ticket purchase.
- Right after the order, offer the thing that solves the follow-up problem.
- If they say no, offer a downsell with a payment plan. Example: a $147 upsell they skip becomes 3 payments of $49 for the same offer.
- Use the confirmation page for the next step, for example an invitation to a free call to see if they need more support.

## 4. Cross sell your customers (short-term CLV)

- Invite customers into a community to hang out.
- Give more free value than others charge for.
- Encourage them to share wins, ask questions and help each other.
- Build a community of like-minded people and care about them.
- Offer related products or services that fit their goals. Example: someone who bought funnel design might also want to learn copywriting, attend an event or use a piece of software.

## 5. Ascend your customers (long-term CLV)

By now you've solved a few of their problems with free and low-ticket offers, so they trust you and see you as the expert.

- Find the most valuable outcome you can deliver for them.
- Create a high-ticket back-end offer: 1:1 access or done-for-you.
- Launch an application funnel with both a form and a calendar, so you know them before you talk.
- Raise the price to attract premium clients. High standards make the work more enjoyable.
- Use testimonials and case studies from your earlier offers.
- Use email to stay top of mind, then invite people to the back-end offer.

## 6. Retain your customers (CRR)

- Offer subscriptions to build monthly recurring revenue.
- Offer payment plans when needed, so customers can stay longer.
- Keep improving the products and services.
- Run monthly giveaways or prizes for active community members.
- Go live, engage and connect with the audience regularly.
- Survey customers on what they like and what to improve.

## 7. Ignite your customers to become promoters (CAR)

- Give customers a reason to share your stuff on social media.
- Pay a percentage of the revenue they bring in, as affiliates.
- Turn the best affiliates into brand ambassadors.

## Flywheel review (monthly, Analytics and CMO)

1. Pull the seven numbers for the last 30 days and the 30 before. Profit stays in the private report.
2. Mark the weakest step and the step that moved most.
3. Pick one fix for the weakest step, with an owner and a date.
4. Put the other ideas in `my-business/queue.md`.

```
FLYWHEEL REVIEW: [month]
Find (TAM):          [reach or new leads]     vs last month [+/-]
Convert (CVR):       [%]                      [+/-]
Upsell (AOV):        [amount]                 [+/-]
Cross sell (CLV-S):  [repeat purchase rate]   [+/-]
Ascend (CLV-L):      [buyers who moved up]    [+/-]
Retain (CRR):        [%]                      [+/-]
Ignite (CAR):        [referrals or shares]    [+/-]
Weakest step: [step]. Fix: [one action] -> [agent], due [date]
```
