# Outlier research

An outlier is a piece of content that did far better than that creator usually does. Big channels get big views on everything, so raw views tell you little. The multiple shows what the audience wanted more than usual, and that is what's worth learning from.

The research is always for the owner's niche and the owner's customer from the Brain (sections 1 and 3). Never reuse another business's list.

## The rules

- **Outlier multiple** = the video's views ÷ the median views of the channel's latest uploads (use the last 10 to 30 regular videos, not shorts or live streams).
- **Floor:** at least 2x, and at least 15,000 views (10,000 in a small niche). Raise both in a big niche.
- **Fresh:** published in the last 90 days, unless the owner asks for evergreen topics.
- **Flag possible paid promotion:** extreme multiples on tiny channels, and titles built around a free trial or an affiliate offer. Their views likely came from ads. Keep them out of the top picks or mark them clearly.
- **Dates:** if the platform only shows "3 weeks ago" or "2 months ago", record the month.
- **Posts:** the same idea works on any platform. Use engagement (comments, shares, saves) ÷ the median of that account's recent posts.

## Build the pool

1. Start from the Creator agent's watchlist (`my-business/team/creator.md`): creators and founders the owner's customer already watches.
2. Add channels that teach the same customer in nearby niches. A fitness coach for busy parents can learn from parenting, productivity and meal-planning channels.
3. Aim for 50 to 150 channels. More channels find more outliers, fewer channels keep it relevant.

How to collect the numbers, in order of preference:

1. **An official API.** The YouTube Data API gives views and upload dates for a channel's latest videos with a free key from the Google Cloud console. Add the key to the AI tool's connections, never to a file in the repo.
2. **An AI tool that can browse.** Ask it to open each channel's videos page, sorted by latest, and read the numbers.
3. **By hand.** Ten channels take about twenty minutes. Paste the numbers in and the agent does the math.

Use each platform as its terms allow, and only public numbers.

## Tag the format

Every outlier gets one format. These 22 come from a study of 332 outliers across about 160 business and AI channels (November 2026), most common first. Each has a title pattern you can fit to any niche.

| # | Format | Title pattern |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | New tool tested | I tested [NEW TOOL] for [TASK]. Here's my verdict |
| 2 | News reaction | [COMPANY] just [CHANGED THING]. What it means for [AUDIENCE] |
| 3 | Tutorial or how-to | How to [TASK] for beginners ([YEAR]) |
| 4 | Big-number use-case list | [NUMBER] ways to use [TOOL OR SKILL] in [YEAR] |
| 5 | Platform tutorial or news | How to [SPECIFIC TASK] in [PLATFORM] |
| 6 | Interview or podcast | [EXPERT]: [SURPRISING CLAIM] |
| 7 | Platform shift warning | The new era of [THING] has begun |
| 8 | Contrarian "instead of" | What I'd learn instead of [POPULAR THING] |
| 9 | AI runs a business task | My AI agents now [BUSINESS TASK]. Here's how |
| 10 | Mindset or story | [BIG CHANGE] is coming. Here's what people will still pay for |
| 11 | Start over or year plan | If I started over with zero [ASSET], here's how I'd [GOAL] in [TIME] |
| 12 | Insider workflow reveal | How [TOP PERSON OR COMPANY] actually uses [TOOL] |
| 13 | Teardown with numbers | Can I fix this failing [BUSINESS] in [DAYS] days? |
| 14 | Steal this | [NUMBER] [TEMPLATES OR PROMPTS] worth stealing |
| 15 | Compressed expertise | Learn 80% of [SKILL] in [MINUTES] minutes |
| 16 | Side income after hours | [NUMBER] [SKILL] side projects you can do after work |
| 17 | Extreme experiment | I did [THING] 100 times. Here's what happened |
| 18 | Upgrade list | Once you [MILESTONE], upgrade these [NUMBER] things |
| 19 | Identity | If you [TRAIT], please start [THING] |
| 20 | Reverse engineer | I reverse engineered [SUCCESSFUL THING] |
| 21 | Debate or challenge | [TYPE A] vs [TYPE B]: the [TOPIC] debate |
| 22 | Paid-for secret | I paid [EXPERT] for [SECRET]. Here's what I learned |

Formats 13, 16 and 22 make money claims. Use them only with real numbers from the proof bank, and follow house rule 11 in brain/CORE.md (proof and the income disclaimer).

## Deliver the list

```
OUTLIERS: week of [date]   Niche: [from the Brain]   Pool: [n] channels
| Title | Channel | Views | Channel median | Multiple | Month | Format | Flag |
...
Top 5 for [avatar]:
1. [title] ([multiple]) | Why it worked: [promise, hook, packaging] | Our version: [title in the owner's words] | Pyramid: [level] | CONTENT: [letter]
...
Patterns this week: [what 3 or more outliers have in common]
```

"Our version" keeps the format and the promise that worked, and uses the owner's story, example, opinion and proof. Never copy a title, script or thumbnail. The plan goes to [content-system](../content-system/SKILL.md), and the hooks go to the Copywriter.

An example of the finished output, for one niche: [the outlier videos gallery](https://outlier-videos.vibepreview.app/), with notes in [galleries/outlier-videos.md](../../galleries/outlier-videos.md). Its videos are for that niche only, so run your own list.
