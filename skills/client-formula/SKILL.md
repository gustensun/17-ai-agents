# The Client Formula

For owners who sell a service. Three phases with three steps each. The aim is to sell outcomes, and to turn one-off projects into long-term partnerships.

| Phase | Steps | In one line |
|---|---|---|
| Position | 1 Market, 2 Offer, 3 Portfolio | Pick a niche. Create a full-stack offer and create examples to show prospects. |
| Promotion | 4 Magnets, 5 Outreach, 6 Inbound | Create lead magnets to find leads, then offer freemium no-risk, no-effort test projects. |
| Partner | 7 Close, 8 Retain, 9 Ascend | Turn one-off projects into recurring revenue, and potentially a % of sales or profit. |

## Phase 1: Position

**1. Market.** A growing market with purchasing power. Clients with several offers need several [SERVICE] projects, so they come back. Pick a market you care about, because you'll spend years in it. A dream client in the wrong business is still a nightmare client.

**2. Offer.** A productized service plus software for monthly recurring revenue, in three parts:

| Part | What it is | Example for a funnel builder |
|---|---|---|
| 01 Full-stack build | Everything needed to get the outcome, fast | Launch their funnels in 7 days: market research, funnel strategy, offer and ascension paths, branding, conversion design, copywriting, the build in [FUNNEL SOFTWARE], tech and automations, CRO |
| 02 Maintenance and optimization | Manage the tech and improve it every month | A/B split tests, front-end offer tests with ads, email marketing and deliverability, rebranding, site updates, launching new offers |
| 03 Operating system | AI agents and automations that do the work, running on software the client keeps using | Their CRM, pipelines and agents set up in [CRM], which is what makes it sticky |

Use AI for most of the delivery, and price on the outcome. The CMO shapes it with [winning-offer-formula](../winning-offer-formula/SKILL.md).

**3. Portfolio.** Projects that show what you can build, on one page:

1. A hero promise: who you help and the outcome.
2. Concept projects: examples you built for your niche before you had clients there.
3. Client projects, with results where you have them.
4. Your own sites and funnels.
5. About you and your process.
6. One call to action: book a call or send a message.
7. An FAQ.

## Phase 2: Promotion

Get in front of the right clients, consistently.

**4. Magnets.** Free audits, templates or tools that attract ideal clients. Two-step promo posts: offer the free thing in a post, then send it to the people who ask for it, which starts a conversation. Find leads in other people's communities by helping first, within each community's rules. Build your own newsfeed: a list of people, groups and hashtags you check every day for people asking for help.

**5. Outreach.** Send a personalized gift before you ask for anything: a short audit, a redesign of one section, a template for their niche. Make it no-risk and no-effort for them. Show what you did instead of saying what you'll do. The Copywriter and Sales agent use [dm-closing-scripts](../dm-closing-scripts/SKILL.md).

**6. Inbound.** Content and referrals that bring clients to you, and compound over time. Post about current projects, client testimonials, behind the scenes of your builds, personal updates and lessons. Plan it with [content-system](../content-system/SKILL.md).

## Phase 3: Partner

**7. Close.** Audit the prospect. Become the consultant. Prescribe like a doctor: ask before you price. Sell the vision, then agree on scope, price and start date. Use the 3C's (Connect, Collect, Close) and a one-line price anchor close. It works in DMs and on calls. Stay detached from the sale.

**8. Retain.** Run the client's business on your system, so you stay part of it every month and see where else you can help. Suggest new projects when you spot an opportunity.

**9. Ascend.** Get the client quick wins first. Then offer an ongoing service fee, or a fee plus a percentage of the revenue you add. This step suits an owner who wants to grow an agency. An owner who wants a lifestyle one-person business can stop at the productized service, the software and digital products.

The CMO owns Position, Lead Gen and Content own Promotion, Sales owns Close, and the CRM agent tracks Retain and Ascend in the pipeline stages from the CRM card.
