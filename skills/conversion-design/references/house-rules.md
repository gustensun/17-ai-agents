# House Rules

Paste this block once into your build tool's rules file, project instructions or custom instructions. If the tool has no such place, paste it at the bottom of every build request. The Design, Funnel and Coding agents include it in every brief they send to a builder.

```
HOUSE RULES (follow on every page, every edit)

COPY
1. Write for one person: the avatar in the Brain (my-business/BRAIN.md). Use their words.
2. Plain, direct, short sentences. One idea per sentence. Mix in one longer sentence per section so it doesn't read like a drumbeat.
3. Every headline makes a concrete promise or names a concrete problem. No vague headlines.
4. Never invent proof. Prices, results, numbers, testimonials, client names, logos, ratings, dates and guarantees go in [SQUARE BRACKETS] unless they are in the Brain.
5. No em dashes or en dashes. Use full stops, commas, colons or brackets.
6. Banned words: unlock, elevate, seamless, supercharge, revolutionize, revolutionary, game-changer, next-level, cutting-edge, empower, harness, leverage (as a verb), delve, embark, journey (unless it's travel), landscape, ecosystem, effortless, robust, world-class, best-in-class, transformative.
7. Banned shapes: "It's not X, it's Y". "Whether you're A or B". "Imagine a world where". "Say goodbye to". "Ready to transform your X?". A question answered right away ("The result? ..."). Three neat stats in a row under the hero.
8. Turn features into impact: the feature, then what it lets them do, then what changes in their life.
9. Buttons say a verb plus what they get ("Get the checklist", "Book my call"). Never "Submit", "Learn more" or "Click here".

CONVERSION
10. One page, one job, one action. Every main button goes to the same next step. No competing links in the hero. Hide nav links on funnel pages (keep them only on a website home page).
11. The main button is solid, high contrast and the same style everywhere. It passes 4.5:1 contrast. Secondary actions are text links, never a second solid button.
12. The hero says what it is, who it's for and what they get, with the main button visible without scrolling on desktop and mobile.
13. Proof sits next to the claims and buttons it supports. A proof slot ([TESTIMONIAL] or a real result) within one scroll of every main button.
14. Lead with outcomes. Cards and lists start with what the reader gets. Don't build the page from rows of equal icon + heading + text cards.
15. Price blocks in this order: what they get, the real value (only if true), the price, payment options, the guarantee, the button. No hidden fees, no fake "was" prices.
16. Urgency only if it's real (a real deadline, seat limit or price rise). No fake countdown timers.
17. No fake testimonials, no stock photos posing as customers, no fake star ratings, no fake "as seen on" logos. Leave labelled slots instead.
18. Forms ask only what's needed. Opt-in: first name + email. Every field has a visible label.

DESIGN
19. Follow the Style Block exactly: direction, fonts, palette and motif.
20. 6 colours max. 2 font families max. One button style. One card style. One corner radius scale.
21. No generic purple or blue gradients, no gradient text, no glass for decoration, no emoji as icons, no centred-everything layouts, no small uppercase label above section headings, no 01/02/03 section numbers unless the order matters. One pre-headline on the hero is fine when it does a job in a full phrase: it calls out the reader or names the category.
22. Real photos where they exist. Generated images only for scenes, never fake people posing as customers.
23. One section per screen. Generous space. Equal left and right edges.

MOTION
24. One signature moment per page, small hover feedback on every button and card, one entrance on the hero. Animate transform and opacity only. Respect prefers-reduced-motion with a still page. Content is visible even if JavaScript fails.

BUILD
25. Mobile first. Check 390px wide before desktop. Tap targets at least 44px. No sideways scroll.
26. AA contrast, visible focus styles, alt text on images, labels on every input, one H1 per page.
27. Fast: compress images, lazy-load below the fold, set width and height on images, 2 font families, no heavy libraries for small effects.
28. All colours and fonts live in CSS variables or design tokens, so the brand changes in one place.
29. Error messages say what went wrong and what to do next, with a link to the place to fix it.
30. When you finish, list every [BRACKET] left on the page so the owner can fill them in.
```

When a builder keeps breaking the rules or undoing fixes, use fix F14 in [fixes.md](fixes.md).
