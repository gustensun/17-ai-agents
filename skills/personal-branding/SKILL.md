# Personal branding

Twelve principles that decide whether people trust you enough to pay you well, and whether you stand out from everyone selling something similar. Each has one test question. The answer is how the market sees you today, which can differ from how you see yourself.

| # | Principle | Test question | What builds it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Personality | Are you perceived as a likable person? | Showing who you are: kind, caring, confident, empathetic. An authentic personality makes you stand out and feel approachable. |
| 2 | Authority | Are you speaking from a place of expertise? | Expertise shown through talks, articles, posts and industry events. Language matters, confidence can be felt, and real-life experience beats generic information every time. |
| 3 | Positioning | Are you a commodity or a crucial part of their growth? | A clear, consistent way of presenting yourself that sets you apart. Compare "[SERVICE] provider" with "growth partner": the same skills, a very different place in the client's business. |
| 4 | Credibility | What results do you have for yourself or others? | Results for yourself and others, expertise in one industry, giving value and keeping promises. Results compound over time. When you're starting out, results can be visual (before and after, the work itself) instead of data. |
| 5 | Pricing | Would someone with 10+ years of experience charge your fees? | Price signals value and commitment to quality. Specialists charge for experience and outcome. Beginners charge for time. Once you've left the beginner phase, stop pricing like one. |
| 6 | Relevance | Are you speaking their language and do you share the same values? | Topics and problems your audience cares about, their industry terms, visuals they recognize. |
| 7 | Engagement | Are you being validated by a community, or do you look desperate? | Real conversations: replying to comments, answering questions, joining discussions. Engagement from others is validation. Chasing it looks desperate. |
| 8 | Framework | Are you a copycat, or have you built something unique and proven? | A named set of principles or steps that guides your work. It shows you built something from real experience. |
| 9 | Portfolio | Can you show examples of your BPW (Best Possible Work)? | A collection of your best work and results, shown visually wherever the work is visual. |
| 10 | Association | Are you respected and associated with the greats? | Relationships with respected people and brands in your industry. Big-name clients are key, even if the first project is free. |
| 11 | Endorsement | Do you have testimonials, referrals or endorsements from others? | Testimonials and referrals from happy clients and customers, shared where buyers decide. |
| 12 | Celebrity | Are you seen as a celebrity, or a famous person, in the eyes of your audience? | Recognition in your field: PR, podcasts, events, collaborations, being the name people mention. |

## Run the audit

1. Read Brain sections 1 (you and the brand), 5 (offer and price), 7 (story and proof bank) and 9 (competition). Look at the owner's public profiles, site and recent content if the owner shares the links.
2. Score each principle from 1 to 5 based on what a stranger can see in two minutes, then write one sentence of evidence for the score.
3. Pick the three lowest scores that matter most for the current goal (Q6). A founder selling a service usually needs Positioning, Portfolio and Endorsement first. Someone growing an audience usually needs Personality, Relevance and Framework first.
4. Give one next action per pick, small enough to finish this week, with the agent who does it.

## What the team builds from it

- **CMO:** the positioning line ("I help [WHO] get [OUTCOME] with [METHOD]") and the price check against principle 5.
- **Copywriter:** bios for each profile, the about section, the story in three lengths from the epiphany (Q17).
- **Design:** the portfolio page and profile visuals from the style block.
- **Content:** posts that show personality, framework and endorsements, tagged in the [content-system](../content-system/SKILL.md).
- **Lead Gen and Sales:** association targets, the people and brands worth working with first, even for free.

Every claim in a bio or portfolio follows house rules 1 and 11 in brain/CORE.md: only real results, clients and endorsements, with permission.
