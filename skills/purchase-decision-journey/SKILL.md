# Purchase Decision Journey

A buyer starts at Point A, their current situation, and wants Point B, their dream destination. The space between is the gap, and the offer is the bridge across it. Nobody crosses because a page is pretty. They cross when they hold twelve beliefs, earned in six stages. This is the new way to sell: evidence-based conversion funnels, where every section earns a belief with proof.

The stage table and the twelve beliefs, word for word, are in [brain/CORE.md](../../brain/CORE.md#the-purchase-decision-journey). This skill shows how to use them.

## The six stages

| Stage | Awareness | The buyer's question | Your job | Beliefs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Desire | Opportunity aware: the dream destination | Is a better life possible here? | Paint the picture | 1 |
| Pain | Problem aware: the current situation | Does this person understand my situation? | Show empathy | 2, 5 |
| Hope | Process aware: the process to the solution | Is there a way that works for someone like me? | Explain logically | 3, 6 |
| Belief | Source aware: trust the person | Can this person get me there? | Introduce credibility | 4, 7 |
| Risk | Offer aware: a convenient experience | Is it worth the money, time and risk? | Sell convenience | 8, 9, 10 |
| Action | Prio aware: prioritize action | What do I do, and why now? | Make it a priority | 11, 12 |

## Where each belief gets earned

| # | Belief | On a sales page (17P sections) | In content | On a call or in DMs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Outcome | Promise, Perfect Persona | Empowering posts, customer wins | "What would reaching that change for you?" |
| 2 | Problem | Problem, Pain Points | Posts that name the exact moment it hurts | "What's it costing you right now?" |
| 3 | Solution | Process to Outcome, Positioning of Offer | Educational posts, new discoveries | "Have you seen this approach before?" |
| 4 | You | Personal Epiphany, Proof of Results | Your story, thought leadership | Share one relevant result of your own |
| 5 | Myself | Problem (why the old way failed them) | Myth-busting, the common enemy | "What have you tried so far, and what happened?" |
| 6 | Your process | Process to Outcome, Product Breakdown | Frameworks, behind the scenes | Walk through the steps in plain words |
| 7 | Hope | Proof from Others, Project Spotlight | Customer results, case studies | A result from someone with their exact situation |
| 8 | Financial | Price Anchor, Pricing Tables, Product Bonuses | The cost of the problem in numbers | "What would it be worth to fix this?" |
| 9 | Time | Product Breakdown (time needed), FAQ | Short demos of how little time it takes | "How much time can you give this a week?" |
| 10 | Risk | Guarantee in the pricing table, Pushback Reflex, FAQ | Objection posts | Name the risk, then the cost of waiting |
| 11 | Next steps | Promotional Bar, final call to action | Posts that end with one clear step | "Here's exactly what happens after you say yes" |
| 12 | Urgency | Priority | Launch posts with real dates | A real deadline, or the cost of another month |

## Match the page to the stage

- **Opt-in pages** run on intrigue and desire: beliefs 1 to 3. Short, one promise, one form.
- **Sales pages** run on trust and priority: beliefs 3 to 12, with proof beside every claim.
- **Applications and calls** run on authority and exclusivity: beliefs 4, 7, 10 and 11, plus a clear "who this is for".
- **Cold traffic** starts at belief 1. **Warm audiences** often hold 1 to 4 already, so lead with the offer and proof.

## Belief audit

Use it on any page, funnel, email sequence or call script before launch, and on every page that converts below target.

1. Read the piece as the avatar from the Brain, top to bottom.
2. For each belief, find the line or section that earns it and the proof behind it.
3. Mark it **earned** (claim plus proof), **claimed** (said without proof) or **missing**.
4. Fix the missing ones first, starting with the earliest stage. A buyer who doesn't hold belief 2 never reads the price.
5. Turn every claimed belief into an earned one with proof from the Brain, or a [PROOF: what kind] placeholder.

Output:

```
BELIEF AUDIT: [page name]
1 Outcome: earned. Hero promise + [result] in the subheadline.
2 Problem: earned. Pain Points section, avatar's words from the Avatar grid.
5 Myself: missing. Add 3 lines on why the old way failed them (Brain section 4).
8 Financial: claimed. "Worth 10x the price" has no math. Add the cost of the problem.
Fix first: 5, then 8.
```

## Rules

- Proof only from the Brain. Missing proof is a placeholder, never an invention.
- Urgency (belief 12) only when it's real: a true deadline, a real seat limit, a real price change.
- One belief can be earned by one strong section. Don't stuff all twelve into a short page; earn the ones its reader is missing.
