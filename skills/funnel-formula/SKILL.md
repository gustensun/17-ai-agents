# The Funnel Formula

Nine assets in three phases. Each phase feeds the next, so build them in order. When something is unclear, the agent asks the owner instead of guessing (the interview in [context-interview](../context-interview/SKILL.md)).

| Phase | Steps | What AI does here |
|---|---|---|
| Define | 1 Context, 2 Brand, 3 Offer | Use AI to build your Brand DNA |
| Design | 4 Strategy, 5 Copy, 6 Graphics | Use AI to generate the funnel |
| Deploy | 7 Automations, 8 Traffic, 9 CRO | Use AI to launch and scale |

## Define

**1. Context.** The 40 setup questions, answered and saved in `my-business/BRAIN.md`: the epiphany story, the unique vehicle, the problem and pain points, the transformation, what customers tried before, why it didn't work and how this is different. Owner: COO with the setup interview.

**2. Brand.** Positioning, values, and the brand and visual identity. To get a brand kit fast, give the AI screenshots of the brand's site or posts and ask it to pull the colors, fonts, spacing and tone into a one-page brand sheet you can reuse on every project. Owner: CMO and Design.

**3. Offer.** Pricing, the offer stack and real urgency. Build it with [winning-offer-formula](../winning-offer-formula/SKILL.md). Owner: CMO.

## Design

**4. Strategy.** The funnel type and every funnel step. Pick it with [funnel-strategy](../funnel-strategy/SKILL.md) and map the pages with [funnel-types](../funnel-types/SKILL.md). Example: an automated cart funnel for a $297 program sold to an organic audience. Owner: Funnel.

**5. Copy.** Narrative framing. Pick the big idea, the source (why this works and who it comes from) and the objection handling, then make every section build that narrative and hold the frame. The copy overcomes three types of limiting belief:

- **Product:** doubts about the thing itself. "Will this work for my offer?" "Which software do I need?"
- **People:** doubts about themselves and past experiences. "I'm not a techy person." "I've wasted money before."
- **Priority:** doubts about timing and budget. "I'm so busy already." "I have bills coming up."

The offer side of these is in the objection handling offer hacks in [winning-offer-formula](../winning-offer-formula/SKILL.md).

Write it with [17p-sections](../17p-sections/SKILL.md), check it with [conversions-framework](../conversions-framework/SKILL.md) and the twelve beliefs in `brain/CORE.md`. Owner: Copywriter.

**6. Graphics.** Conversion design. Branding builds trust, and the design communicates what you're selling. Design from the finished copy: the sections come from the 17P's, same as the copy, and visual hierarchy helps people take the page in. Use [conversion-design](../conversion-design/SKILL.md). Owner: Design.

## Deploy

**7. Automations.** Domain, payments, forms, the autoresponder and message sequencing (email and text). Owner: Automations.

**8. Traffic.** Cold traffic or warm traffic, launch or evergreen. The plan comes from [funnel-strategy](../funnel-strategy/SKILL.md), the content from [content-system](../content-system/SKILL.md), the ads from [winning-ad-formula](../winning-ad-formula/SKILL.md). Owner: CMO with Creator and Content.

**9. CRO.** Split testing, in this order: headlines and wording first, the hero section second, then the layout, page length, video or no video, writing style, section order, where the proof sits and the graphic style. One change per test. Owner: Analytics with Funnel.

## The ascension path

Four levels, and most businesses need all four. A beginner with no offer yet starts at level 1. A beginner with an offer starts at level 2 and adds level 1 to feed it.

| Level | Offer | Job | What makes it work |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Freebies | Get leads | Custom tools and discoveries instead of PDFs: a calculator, a planner, a quiz with a real result |
| 02 | Low-ticket bundles | Turn leads into buyers | Wow them with a product so useful it sells them on you, and let it pay for ads |
| 03 | Mid-ticket products | Sell the full transformation with no 1:1 work | Method, moat and market from [digital-product-fastlane](../digital-product-fastlane/SKILL.md) |
| 04 | High-ticket back end | Ascend your best buyers | More help for the people who already got a result, closed in DMs or on a short call ([dm-closing-scripts](../dm-closing-scripts/SKILL.md)) |

For a high-ticket offer, a one-page written proposal (scope, price, start date, next step) in a shared doc often replaces the sales call.

## How the COO runs this as a project

1. Check which of the nine assets already exist in `my-business/` and which are missing.
2. Write one handoff note per missing asset, in phase order, to the owner listed above.
3. Hold Design until the copy is approved. Hold Deploy until the owner approves the pages.
4. After launch, hand step 9 to Analytics with a weekly test plan.

```
FUNNEL FORMULA STATUS: [offer]
Define:  Context [done/missing]  Brand [ ]  Offer [ ]
Design:  Strategy [ ]  Copy [ ]  Graphics [ ]
Deploy:  Automations [ ]  Traffic [ ]  CRO [ ]
Next asset: [name] -> [agent], due [date]
Waiting on the owner: [one question, or none]
```
