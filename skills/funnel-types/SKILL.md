# Funnel types

Pick the type with [funnel-strategy](../funnel-strategy/SKILL.md) first. Then build from the page map below. Every page has one job and one action. If a page has two jobs, split it.

Each type starts with its three core steps in bold, then the full page map, including the optional pages most funnels of that type end up needing. The first seven are the funnels used most for coaches, creators, service businesses and digital products. Ecom is for physical products.

## 1. Website funnel

Stage: get leads. **Homepage** (your brand and content) > **Lead capture** (a freebie or next step) > **Nurture** (emails that build trust).

A home page that works as the hub every other funnel plugs into. It shows who you help and what changes, then gets the opt-in. Good for local businesses, coaches, consultants, speakers, authors and agencies.

| Page | Its one job | The one action |
|---|---|---|
| Home page | Show who you help and what changes for them | Get the free [LEAD MAGNET] |
| Opt-in popup | Turn interest into an email | Send me the [LEAD MAGNET] |
| Thank-you and delivery | Deliver the freebie, make a soft offer | Open it, then see the invitation |
| Invitation page (optional) | Invite the warmest leads to work with you | Apply or book a call |
| Confirmation | Lock in the next step | Add to calendar |

## 2. Lead funnel

Stage: get leads. **Opt-in page** (a free tool or discovery) > **Thank-you page** (offer the next step) > **Email sequence** (nurture into the offer).

The front door for cold traffic and the first level of the ascension path. People want custom tools and discoveries more than another PDF: a calculator, a planner, a quiz with a real result, a template they can use today.

| Page | Its one job | The one action |
|---|---|---|
| Opt-in page | Trade one specific result for an email | Get instant access |
| Thank-you page | Deliver the freebie and offer the next step | Open the tool, then the low-ticket offer |
| Email sequence | Get the first win, then sell the next step | One link per email |

Curiosity copy works here. Trust needed is low, so keep the page short.

## 3. Low-ticket funnel

Stage: get buyers. **Sales page** (one irresistible bundle) > **Checkout and bump** (add an order bump) > **Upsell** (ascend while they buy) > Confirmation.

A small, easy-yes product (usually $7 to $47) that turns strangers into buyers and pays for ads. Good for kits, template packs, mini courses, toolkits and workshop recordings.

| Page | Its one job | The one action |
|---|---|---|
| Sales page | Sell the front-end offer with a no-brainer stack | Get instant access |
| Order form and bump | Take the payment, add the bump | Complete my order |
| Upsell 1 (one-time offer) | Offer the next logical step at a special price | Yes, add this to my order |
| Downsell | A smaller version or a payment plan for people who said no | Yes, I'll take this instead |
| Upsell 2 (optional) | The next rung up | Yes, add this |
| Confirmation | Access, first win, next step | Access my product |

Each step should be the next thing a buyer of the previous step wants. The bump sits above the pay button and is never pre-ticked. Show the price near the first button. Click-test the whole purchase path before you send traffic.

## 4. Webinar cart funnel

Stage: get buyers. **Registration** (sign up for the training) > **Live webinar** (teach, then make the offer) > **Cart open** (limited time to buy) > Confirmation.

People register for a free live or automated training, watch it and buy from a cart during or after it. Good for offers that need teaching first: courses, group programs, coaching, software, mid to high ticket.

| Page | Its one job | The one action |
|---|---|---|
| Registration | Get the registration | Save my seat |
| Registration confirmation | Make sure they show up | Add to calendar |
| Webinar room | Teach, then make the offer | Get [OFFER] (appears at the offer) |
| Replay | Catch the no-shows and fence-sitters | Watch, then buy |
| Order form | Take the payment | Complete my order |
| Confirmation | Onboard | Access or book onboarding |

Real dates and real cart close times only. Reminder emails and texts decide the show-up rate.

## 5. VSL application funnel

Stage: higher ticket. Opt-in > **VSL page** (the video sells the vision) > **Application** (qualify who's a fit) > **Close** (on a call or in DMs).

A short opt-in, then a video sales letter that sells the result and you, then an application and a calendar. Close on a call, or in DMs once they know you. Good for high ticket: coaching, consulting, done-for-you, agency services, masterminds.

| Page | Its one job | The one action |
|---|---|---|
| Opt-in | Get the email for the free training | Watch the free training |
| VSL page | Deliver the training and invite the right people | Apply now |
| Application and calendar | Qualify, then book | Book my call |
| Not-a-fit page | Help people who don't qualify | Get [DOWNSELL OFFER] |
| Confirmation | Get them to show up ready | Watch this before our call |

Price usually stays off the page. Qualification must be real: if anyone can book, the application adds friction without adding authority.

## 6. Automated cart funnel

Stage: higher ticket. **Sales page** (long-form copy or VSL) > **Checkout** (no sales calls needed) > **Upsells** (upsell, downsell, thank you).

A long-form sales page that sells a considered purchase (a course, program, membership or bundle) with no sales calls. The buyer thinks longer than on a low-ticket offer, so the page makes the full argument, payment plans matter and follow-up emails do a lot of the selling.

| Page | Its one job | The one action |
|---|---|---|
| Sales page | Make the full case with all the 17P sections that apply | Join now |
| Order form | Take the payment, offer a plan | Complete my order |
| Upsell | The next step at a one-time price | Yes, upgrade my order |
| Downsell | A lighter or split-payment version | Yes, I'll take this |
| Confirmation and onboarding | Access and first win | Start lesson 1 |
| Follow-up emails | Bring back people who didn't buy | Back to the sales page |

## 7. Challenge funnel

Stage: higher ticket. **Registration** (join the free challenge) > **The challenge** (seven days of quick wins) > **The offer** (pitch on the final day).

A free or low-cost multi-day challenge. People sign up, get offered a paid VIP upgrade, show up each day for a quick win and get invited to the main offer on the final day. Good for launches that need buzz and trust fast.

| Page | Its one job | The one action |
|---|---|---|
| Landing page | Pitch the challenge, get the sign-up | Join the free challenge |
| Opt-in popup | Get the email | Save my spot |
| VIP upgrade | Offer a paid upgrade right after sign-up | Upgrade to VIP |
| VIP order form | Take the VIP payment | Complete my upgrade |
| Confirmation | Get them ready and into the group | Join the group, add to calendar |
| Day pages | Deliver the day, get the task done | Do today's task |
| Offer page | Sell the main offer on the final day | Join [OFFER] |

Plan the days before the pages: each day gets one quick win and sets up one belief the offer needs. A challenge with a fake start date kills trust.

## 8. Ecom funnel

Stage: get buyers. **Product sales page** > **Checkout and bump** > **Upsell** > Confirmation.

One hero product on its own sales page instead of a store with 200 items, a fast checkout with a bump and a one-click upsell after purchase.

| Page | Its one job | The one action |
|---|---|---|
| Product sales page | Sell the hero product or bundle | Buy now |
| Checkout and bump | Take the order, add a small extra | Complete my order |
| Post-purchase upsell | One-click add-on that fits the order | Yes, add to my order |
| Confirmation | Reassure and set expectations | Track my order |

Product photos decide this funnel. Shipping, returns and delivery times must be real and match on every page. Never show a rating that isn't real.

## Rules for every funnel

- One job and one action per page. Every button on a page goes to the same next step.
- Write the copy first with [17p-sections](../17p-sections/SKILL.md), then design from the copy with [conversion-design](../conversion-design/SKILL.md).
- Only real proof, real prices, real deadlines and real scarcity. Use `[TESTIMONIAL]`, `[RESULT]` and `[VALUE]` slots until the owner gives real ones.
- Every "no thanks" link goes somewhere useful: a downsell, the confirmation page or a free community.
- After each step, name what happens behind it: the email, the tag, the pipeline stage. The Automations agent builds those.
- Test the full path on a phone before traffic: every button, the bump, the upsell yes and no, the confirmation email and the access link.
