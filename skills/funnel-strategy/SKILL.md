# Funnel strategy

Answer three questions before you build anything:

1. **The price point.** What is the price of the product you're selling?
2. **The traffic temperature.** How warmed up to you are they?
3. **The launch format.** How are you launching the offer to your audience?

The answers pick the funnel type. The page list for each type is in [funnel-types](../funnel-types/SKILL.md).

## Why it matters: cash flow economics

An old direct response rule: whoever can spend the most to acquire a customer wins. A customer, once acquired, is easy to keep selling to.

The catch: if you spend more than you make, you lose money. Spend $1,000 a day on ads and make $500 back, and you're down roughly $15k a month. Only investor-backed companies can lose money up front for long. Everyone else needs organic customers, or a funnel that at least breaks even.

Break-even is a strong place to be. Sell a $9 product with two upsells so the average order reaches $100, and every ad dollar comes back while you build a list of buyers. Next month those buyers buy more: more of the same, new products, or more access to you. Those are back-end sales, the things you sell once people are in your world. The math for this is in [seven-figure-math](../seven-figure-math/SKILL.md).

## 1. The price point

| Price | Funnel | How it sells |
|---|---|---|
| $0, free | Lead funnel | A simple opt-in page with curiosity-based copy. Less trust needed: all they risk is their time and contact details. |
| $1 to $100 | Low-ticket funnel (self-liquidating) | A sales page for something people buy without much thought. The goal is to break even on the front end. Upsells offer the next thing they need, as a one-time deal on the page after the order form. |
| $100 to $1,000 | Automated sales funnel | A strong sales page with a 2 to 3 minute sales video that sells on its own: sales page, order form, confirmation. Works best with an organic audience, where content does the trust building before they see the offer. |
| $1,000 to $10,000 | Webinar, VSL or challenge funnel | People need more time with you before paying this much. A free training, workshop, webinar or five-day challenge gives you time to break limiting beliefs and build new ones. A long run of helpful content can do the same job. Answer final questions in DMs when they know you, or on a short call when they don't. |
| $10,000 to $100,000 | Application funnel | Positioning: "we don't work with everyone." An application adds authority, and people want what feels exclusive. Qualification must be real. A call to meet and go over everything fits this price. |

## 2. The traffic temperature

**Cold traffic** (unqualified leads) just found you. Give away more valuable free stuff than others charge for, so trust builds fast: content plus freebies like tools, templates, scripts and trainings. When you sell, start with an offer so good they can't refuse it. Don't sell a $10k offer to someone who just met you. A low-ticket wow offer that gets a real result can build more trust than two years of average content.

**Warm traffic** (qualified leads) already knows you but hasn't bought. They know you, like you and are getting close to trusting you. With consistent free value, some will buy a high-ticket offer when the timing, the trust and the offer line up. Keep a mix of free content and a wow offer everyone in the audience can benefit from, then ascend buyers into other offers on the back end. Promote the other offers regularly so people can join when the time is right.

## 3. The launch format

**Live launch funnels** turn a lot of cold prospects into hot leads fast: live webinars, live five-day challenges, live virtual events, live-streamed sales videos. You give a lot of value up front, make the offer and answer questions live. An open cart launch works too: build an evergreen funnel with a short sales video, then open the cart for 7 to 10 days.

**Evergreen funnels** run all year without you pushing them: automated sales funnels, an automated webinar, a challenge that feels live, a four-part video series. Pick live or evergreen by how the owner likes to build trust and how they want to work. Both need a clear sales page. The webinar, challenge or video series is only the pre-funnel framing that gets people ready to buy.

## Pick the funnel

1. Read the offer, price and value ladder from the Brain (section 5), the launch plan (Q33), the traffic sources from `my-business/team/cmo.md` and how the owner wants to close sales from `my-business/team/sales.md` (calls, DMs or neither). If a setup file is missing, ask the owner those two questions.
2. Answer the three questions in one line each.
3. Recommend one funnel type with three sentences on why. Respect how the owner wants to sell: if they said no calls, design it without calls.
4. Map the customer journey from first attention to ascension: every page in order with its one job and its one call to action, what happens after each step (email, tag, pipeline stage), the next offer for each buyer, and where non-buyers go (nurture, downsell, free community).
5. Run the break-even check with [seven-figure-math](../seven-figure-math/SKILL.md) if ads are part of the plan.
6. Send the recommendation to the owner for approval before the Funnel agent builds.

```
FUNNEL PLAN: [offer]
Price point: [price] -> [funnel type]
Traffic: [cold / warm / both], from [sources]
Launch: [live / open cart / evergreen]
Why this funnel: [three sentences]
Journey: [numbered pages, one job and one call to action each]
After each step: [email, tag, stage]
Next offers: [bump, upsell, back end]
Non-buyers: [nurture, downsell, community]
Break-even CPP: [from the calculator, or "organic only"]
```
