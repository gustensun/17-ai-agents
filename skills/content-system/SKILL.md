# The content system

Content does the selling before anyone sees the offer. Value builds goodwill, and goodwill sells: with an organic audience that trusts you, offers from about $37 to $5,000 and up can sell without sales calls. Most social posts are seen today and gone tomorrow. Search-based video (YouTube) and email keep working for months or years, so the long pieces go there and the short pieces point to them.

## The Pyramid of Content

Four levels, each moving people one step closer to buying. A healthy week has all four.

| Level | It builds | It leads to | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engaging | Discovery | Interest | Hooks, trends, bold opinions, short clips. Gets new people to notice you. |
| Entertaining | Likeability | Connection | Stories, behind the scenes, personality, humor. Gets them to like you. |
| Educational | Trust | Consideration | Tutorials, frameworks, breakdowns, case studies. Gets them to trust you. |
| Empowering | Belief | Decision | Customer wins, your epiphany, "you can do this" proof. Gets them to believe it's possible for them, and decide. |

Lots of engaging content and no empowering content gets views and no buyers. Lots of educational content and no engaging content gets trust from a small audience. Check the mix every week.

## The CONTENT framework

Seven types of idea. When the calendar feels thin, pick a letter.

| Letter | Type | What it is | Example shapes |
|---|---|---|---|
| C | Customer Results | External proof that you get results | A customer's before and after, a case study walkthrough, a review read out loud |
| O | Offer Differentiation | The USP that makes you unique | Your method vs the usual way, why you do it differently, what's inside and why |
| N | New Discoveries | Lessons you're learning | "I tested X for 30 days", what surprised you this week, a mistake and the fix |
| T | Thought Leadership | Controversial truths you believe in | The strong opinions from the Brain (Q16), what the industry gets wrong |
| E | Education and Entertainment | Non-robotic tips with helpful info | One tip with a real example, a tutorial, a myth checked with a test |
| N | Narrative Framing | Stories, metaphors, analogies | Your epiphany story, a customer's story, the analogy that explains your method |
| T | Trends and News | Be the source for industry events | What changed this week and what it means for your customer |

Good topics that come up every week: current projects, customer results, behind the scenes of the work, personal updates and lessons.

## The weekly content plan

Default rhythm, changed in `my-business/team/content.md`:

| Day | Piece | Pyramid level | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Pick 3 ideas from the outlier list and the CONTENT letters, record or write the long piece | Educational or empowering | Content picks, Copywriter scripts |
| Tue | Publish the long piece, email the list about it | Educational | Copywriter, owner approves |
| Wed | Three short clips or posts that point to the long piece | Engaging, entertaining | Content plans, Design makes graphics |
| Thu | Second piece: a comparison, review or customer story | Empowering | Copywriter |
| Fri | Review the numbers with Analytics, pick next week's ideas | | Content, Analytics |

## From outlier to the owner's piece

1. Start from proven ideas: outliers from [outlier-research](../outlier-research/SKILL.md), in the owner's niche, for the owner's customer.
2. Keep the format and the promise that worked. Change everything else: the owner's story, example, opinion and proof. Never copy a script, a title word for word or a thumbnail.
3. Tag each idea with its pyramid level and CONTENT letter.
4. Write the title three ways: a result ("How I got [RESULT] in [TIME]"), a story ("If I started over, here's how I'd [GOAL]") and a question ("Is [THING] still worth it in [YEAR]?"). Promise the outcome, open a loop, or make them click for the answer.
5. Every piece ends with one next step: the freebie, the low-ticket offer or the next video.
6. The Copywriter writes it in the owner's voice and runs the scanner. Design makes the thumbnail or graphic from the style block.

```
CONTENT PLAN: week of [date]
1. [title] | [format] | Pyramid: [level] | CONTENT: [letter] | From outlier: [link] | Next step: [CTA]
2. ...
Mix this week: Engaging [n], Entertaining [n], Educational [n], Empowering [n]
```

## Rules

- Proof in content follows house rules 1 and 11 in brain/CORE.md: real results only, a customer's results only with their permission, and the income disclaimer next to any money claim.
- Nothing publishes without the owner's yes, unless a standing rule in the setup file says otherwise.
- When content gets views but no buyers, add empowering pieces and a clearer next step before making more engaging content.
