# Taste log

Every rating the owner gives goes here, newest first. Agents read the entries with their own name before every task. The COO reads all of them in the weekly upgrade.

How to rate: a number from 1 to 5 and one sentence on why. The why teaches the agent more than the number.

| Date | Agent | Deliverable (file) | Rating | Why | Rule to keep |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| YYYY-MM-DD | copywriter | my-business/drafts/YYYY-MM-DD-optin-page.md | 4 | Strong first line, the proof section was vague | Put the number in the headline when we have one |

## The scale

- **5:** ship it as it is.
- **4:** ship after small edits.
- **3:** right direction, needs a real rewrite.
- **2:** wrong angle or wrong voice.
- **1:** start over. The agent asks one question about what went wrong before it tries again.

## From ratings to rules

When a note says what to do ("I say Hi, not Hello"), the agent writes it as a rule in the "Rule to keep" column and adds it to its setup file (`my-business/team/<agent>.md`) with the revision. The note counts as the owner's yes. When the owner gives the same reason twice without saying what to do, the agent proposes a rule and asks for a yes first. Outputs rated 4 or 5 are copied to `my-business/examples/<agent>/` so the agent can match them next time.
