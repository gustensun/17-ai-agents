## The brief

- **Asset:** the opt-in page for the free 20-Minute Week Planner.
- **Its one job:** trade the planner for a first name and an email.
- **Reader:** a parent from YouTube or Instagram who knows the problem (no free hour) and hasn't seen a plan built for it.
- **Beliefs still missing:** 9 Time (20 minutes fits in nap time) and 3 Solution (three planned sessions beat one lost hour).
- **The one action:** "Send me the planner".

## The pitch, three ways

From the CMO's approved line (Brain section 5, Q14).

1. **Instagram bio:** Strength training for parents of kids under 5. 20 minutes, 3 times a week, around nap time. Free week planner below.
2. **Opt-in subhead:** A free one-page planner for 3 workouts of 20 minutes, with a Plan B day for when nap time doesn't happen.
3. **DM reply to "what do you do?":** I help parents who used to train get strong again at home. 20 minutes, 3 times a week, planned around nap time. Want the free planner? It's how I started.

## Three headlines

1. Plan this week's 3 workouts around nap time
2. 20 minutes nobody can take from you
3. Nap time moves. Your workouts can move with it.

Headline 2 is a parent's own words from the research ("20 minutes nobody can take from me", forum thread, March 2026). Headline 3 answers "nap time lies" (app review, June 2026). All three are under 50 characters, and Design checks the line count at 390px.
