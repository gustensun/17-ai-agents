## The week in numbers

- First drafts: 15 of 17 rated 4 or 5. The two 3s were the COO (too much work for 10 hours) and the Copywriter (a headline that promised a body).
- After one revision: all 17 at 4 or 5.
- 13 rules to keep, now in the agents' setup files in `my-business/team/`.
- Most notes taught the agents something only Dana knew: her hours, her greeting, when her people read email, what she doesn't sell, who she won't chase. One was about facts (old quotes). That's why the training round comes before real work.

## The scale

- **5:** ship it as it is.
- **4:** ship after small edits.
- **3:** right direction, needs a real rewrite.
- **2:** wrong angle or wrong voice.
- **1:** start over. The agent asks one question about what went wrong before it tries again.
