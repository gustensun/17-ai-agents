## 6. Mechanism (Q19, Q21, Q22)

- The process to getting the outcome (Q19), with the steps and the method's name: The Nap Time Method.
  1. Plan the week around naps, not the clock: 3 slots of 20 minutes, plus a Plan B day.
  2. Train the 5 moves that carry over to parent life: squat, hinge, push, pull, carry.
  3. Progress one thing at a time: reps first, then weight, with form checked every week.
  4. Keep the 2-day rule: never miss two planned days in a row. A missed day moves to Plan B.
- The "new opportunity" they haven't tried yet (Q21): Short, planned strength sessions at home that move with the kids' day, instead of hunting for the hour they had before kids.
- How and why your solution is unique to them (Q22): A trainer who is a parent of two built it and did it herself after her second child. Every workout is filmed in a real living room with the interruptions left in, and the plan expects missed days, so one bad night doesn't end the week.
